# Montville Rising & The Visibility Brigade Welcome You

We're a co-branded effort between Montville Rising and the Montville chapter of the Visibility
Brigade — neighbors turning shared concern for our democracy into visible, active participation.
This site has our mission, upcoming events, and how you can get involved.

## What We Do

<div class="pillars">
  <div class="pillar">
    <h3>Civic Engagement</h3>
    <p>Peaceful, nonviolent action and community events that turn concern into participation.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="pillar">
    <h3>Visibility Actions</h3>
    <p>Visible messages in high-traffic spots — like highway overpasses — that spark public discourse.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="pillar">
    <h3>Community</h3>
    <p>A non-partisan, welcoming group for anyone who cares about democracy and the Constitution.</p>
  </div>
</div>
