    <summary>A Win for Montville — Policy 7523 Withdrawn (May 2026)</summary>
    <div class="focus-accordion-body">
      <p>
        The Montville Township Board of Education withdrew the second reading of proposed
        Policy 7523 — a sweeping restriction that would have prohibited district signage from
        acknowledging holidays, civic observances, heritage and recognition months, and PTSA
        fundraising messages.
      </p>
      <p>
        In just over a week, 446 Montville residents signed a petition asking the Board to
        withdraw the policy. On April 28, the Board had advanced it to a second reading on a
        5–4 vote despite no member of the public and no district administrator speaking in
        support. Residents signed, spoke up, and prepared to speak again at the May 12 meeting —
        and the Board withdrew the second reading before that vote could happen.
      </p>
      <p>
        The policy could still return in this form or a revised one. If it does, we're building a
        list of residents willing to attend Board meetings and speak during public comment.
        Email <a href="mailto:montvillerising@gmail.com?subject=Add%20me%20to%20the%20speakers%20list">montvillerising@gmail.com</a>
        with the subject line "Add me to the speakers list" to join.
      </p>
